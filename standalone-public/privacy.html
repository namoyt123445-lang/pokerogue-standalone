Account & save privacy

This independent fan website stores your username, a salted password hash, login sessions, and your uploaded game saves in Cloudflare D1. Passwords are never stored as plaintext. A Secure, HttpOnly cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days.

Game progress and Pokémon are saved to your account automatically while playing online. Saves also remain in this browser. Other people using the same browser profile can access its local data; use a separate browser profile on shared computers. Logging out ends your server session but does not delete local saves.

Guest progress stays separate until you confirm an import. Cloud conflicts require your choice rather than silently overwriting progress. Backups retained on this device can contain your full game progress.

The server also keeps up to three previous cloud-save revisions and your permanent Gems, Daycare slots, eggs, breeding cooldowns, hatch grants, action receipts and deduplicated battle-progress IDs. Restoring a game backup does not roll back this separate Daycare state. Offline victory reports remain in your browser until they can be sent.

Hashed IP-based counters limit abuse. They expire after the rate-limit window and are periodically cleaned up. Cloudflare also processes network traffic under its own policies. This site adds no advertising or analytics trackers.

No email address is collected. Password recovery and self-service account deletion are not available in this release. Keep your password safe. Contact the site owner through the project repository for support; never post passwords or session cookies.

Do not reuse a password from another service. Battles and saves remain client-side and are not cheat-proof. This service is an early fan project, not an official Pokémon account.
